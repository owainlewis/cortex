Coop Defense

Robots are marching on the coop. Put birds on the grass to stop them before they reach the eggs.

  • Tap a bird card, then tap a square to place it.
  • Chicks lay golden seeds. Tap seeds to collect them. You also get a harvest every 10 seconds.
  • Each lane has one guard chick. It charges once if a robot gets through.
  • Clear a wave to unlock a new bird. Recall removes a bird.
  • Keys: 1-8 pick a bird, R recall, P pause.
