Coop Defense

Robots are marching on the coop. Put animals on the grass to stop them before they reach the eggs.

  • Tap an animal card, then tap a square to place it.
  • Chicks lay 30 golden seeds every 5 seconds. Tap seeds to collect them. You also get a harvest every 10 seconds.
  • Each lane has one guard chick. It charges once if a robot gets through.
  • Clearing a wave gives bonus seeds. Recall removes an animal.
  • Keys: 1-8 pick an animal, R recall, P pause.
